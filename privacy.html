2. Information Collected

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect:

  • Appointment Information: Full name, telephone number, email address, chosen haircut/shave services, requested barber, and special preferences.
  • Communications Data: Information submitted through general inquiry forms or emails sent to our reception.
  • Technical Log Data: Anonymized IP addresses, browser user-agents, referring pages, and access timestamps recorded by static hosting infrastructure for network security.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing

We process collected information to:

  • Fulfill and coordinate your grooming appointment requests (contractual necessity).
  • Respond to direct customer service inquiries and support questions.
  • Maintain the operational stability, accessibility, and security of the website.

4. Cookies and Client-Side Storage

This website operates as a lightweight static application. In its default static demonstration deployment, we do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site marketing cookies, tracking beacons, or surveillance analytics.

5. Third-Party Integrations

In a full production deployment, the business owner may connect third-party booking processors (e.g. Acuity Scheduling, Square Appointments, or Mindbody) or communication gateways. When connected, those services process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Security

We implement reasonable physical and technological safeguards to protect personal data against accidental loss or unauthorized access. Demonstration form submissions on this static build are processed strictly within your local browser runtime and are not retained on remote servers.

7. Your Legal Rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, you may hold rights to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your personal records held by BLACKLINE BARBERSHOP. To exercise these rights, please contact our designated privacy officer at privacy@blacklinebarbershop.sample.
